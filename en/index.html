2. Principle

The app stores all data you enter exclusively locally on your device. The provider does not operate a server of its own and has no access to your data. No usage, analytics or advertising data is collected.

3. Data processed (on the device)

  • Trips: date and time, GPS position and recorded route, odometer readings, details of purpose, destination and customer, change log of later edits.
  • Vehicle data: licence plate, optionally the vehicle identification number, odometer reading, Bluetooth ID of the paired OBD adapter.
  • Customer/contact data that you enter yourself (name, address, phone, notes).
  • Keeper/profile data for the logbook printout (name, address, tax number) as well as tax classification details.
  • Saved places (name, coordinates, address).
  • Bluetooth device in the car: name and address of the Bluetooth device (e.g. the hands-free system) that is connected to the phone during your trips, together with a count of how many trips it was present for. The app uses this only on the device to avoid false warnings and does not transmit it. It can be reset under Settings.
  • Technical log (log file): times of connections to the OBD adapter, trip start and end, movement and activity reports of the device, and error messages. It is used for troubleshooting, stays on your device and is only passed on if you share it yourself (e.g. by email to the provider).

Data minimisation: The route is deliberately stored in full because it is needed for the map view, the distance calculation and the evidence of the trips in the logbook. Automatically recorded trips are locked once they are completed to protect the traceability of the logbook and cannot be deleted individually; trips added by hand can be deleted. You delete all data of the app under Settings → “Delete all data” (with a safety prompt). This deletes the data in the app, the automatic backups on the device, the backup folder in your Google Drive (if connected) and the technical log. Backup files that you have stored in a location of your own are to be deleted there by you. Alternatively you delete the data by uninstalling the app or via the app’s storage in the Android settings. No data is collected for advertising, analytics or profiling.

4. Purposes and roles

Processing takes place for your own purposes of keeping a logbook. For personal data of third parties (e.g. your customers) that you enter in the app, you are the controller within the meaning of Art. 4 No. 7 GDPR. The provider of the app does not process this data.

5. Device permissions

  • Location (in the foreground and in the background): for the automatic recording of the route during the trip.
  • Bluetooth: for the connection to the OBD adapter (ignition detection), also to adapters with Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) and as a paired companion device, so that the app learns when the adapter comes into range.
  • Physical activity (activity recognition): The app uses Android’s activity recognition exclusively to detect that you are in a vehicle, so that a trip can be recorded automatically. No steps, fitness or health data are evaluated or stored. The report is only noted locally in the technical log and not transmitted.
  • Notifications: display of the running recording service.
  • Start after device boot and “Alarms & reminders”: so that the recording service is ready again after a restart of the device and keeps running reliably in the background. No data is transmitted in the process.
  • Biometrics/device PIN: only for the optional app lock; the app receives from Android only the result “confirmed” or “not confirmed”, never biometric data.
  • Changing system settings (optional): only if you allow it, so that the display brightness can be raised during the warning “Adapter failed”. The app stores your previous brightness on the device and restores it afterwards. No data is transmitted.

6. External services and data transfers

  • Maps, address and nearby search, route estimation: Coordinates or search terms are transmitted to OpenStreetMap services (map tiles from OpenStreetMap, address search “Nominatim”, nearby search “Overpass”, road course estimation “OSRM”). Operator: OpenStreetMap Foundation, https://osmfoundation.org (based in the United Kingdom). Your IP address is transmitted in the process, and the data may be processed on servers outside the EU. The transmission is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Location determination: takes place through the location services of Google Play services on your device.
  • Optional cloud backup: Only if you activate “Connect Google Drive” under Settings, backup files are uploaded to a folder created by the app in your own Google Drive. The app uses the restricted access “drive.file” and can only see the files it has created itself. Provider: Google Ireland Ltd., Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Without an active connection no upload takes place; the connection can be disconnected at any time in the settings.
  • App updates: The app asks Google Play whether a newer version is available. The app connects to Google Play for this; no trip or customer data is transmitted. Google’s privacy notices apply.
  • QR code check: To check the verification code on the printout, the app uses the code scanner of Google Play services. The camera image is evaluated on the device by the scanner; the app does not store a photo.
  • Your data is not passed on to the provider of the app.

7. Storage period

Your data stays on the device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Cloud backups stay in your Google Drive until you delete them there. Rolling automatic backups are overwritten after the periods stated in the app; the year-end backup is kept permanently to meet tax retention obligations.

8. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Since the data is stored locally on your device, you in practice exercise these rights yourself through the app (export, edit, delete). In addition you have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority; for Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania: Der Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, Schloss Schwerin, Lennéstraße 1, 19053 Schwerin.

9. Data security

The app stores data in the app-private area of the device. An app lock (PIN/biometrics) can optionally be activated. The Android system backup is switched off for the app. Backup files: The backup files of the app (in the “Downloads” folder of your device and, if activated, in your Google Drive) are by default NOT encrypted by the app and are only protected by the protection of your device or your Google account. Under Settings → “Encrypt backups” you can switch on encryption with a password of your own (AES-256, key derivation via PBKDF2). The password is not stored and cannot be reset: without the password, encrypted backups can no longer be read, not even by the provider. Existing unencrypted backups remain, as do the uploaded technical log and, if activated, receipt photos (neither is encrypted). Keep backups carefully and do not share them with unauthorised persons.

10. Purchases through Google Play

The unlocking of the clean printout is purchased through Google Play. Payment data (card, account, address) is processed exclusively by Google; it is neither passed on to the provider nor read by the app. The app receives only the purchase status from Google Play (product ID and purchase confirmation) and stores on your device which logbook years are unlocked. For purchasing and restoring purchases the app connects to Google Play. Google’s privacy notices apply. The provider receives sales and tax information on purchases from Google in its Developer Console, as far as Google provides it.

Terms of use of the app “Fahrtenbuch Pro”

1. Scope

These terms apply to the use of the app “Fahrtenbuch Pro” (hereinafter “app”) of the provider named under “Legal notice”.

2. Description of the service

The app supports keeping a logbook: automatic recording of trips via a Bluetooth OBD adapter and GPS, later assignment of purpose, destination and customer, backup, and export as PDF, Excel, CSV and JSON. The app does not provide tax or legal advice.

3. No assurance of tax recognition

The technical implementation is based on § 6 para. 1 no. 4 EStG (German Income Tax Act), R 8.1 para. 9 LStR (German wage tax guidelines) and the case law on electronic logbooks (among others: prompt, closed keeping; logging of later changes to the core trip data). Whether a specific logbook is accepted is decided by the tax authorities in the individual case on the basis of the actual entries. There is no entitlement to recognition.

4. User’s cooperation

For proper logbook keeping the following are required:

  • correct master data (vehicle, starting odometer reading, customers),
  • prompt assignment of the trips,
  • plausibility check of the recorded routes and odometer readings,
  • regular backup of the data,
  • meeting the technical requirements (compatible ELM327 OBD adapter with classic Bluetooth, GPS switched on, sufficient battery, background operation not restricted by power management, carrying the device in the vehicle).

5. Availability and accuracy

The app depends on device functions (GPS, Bluetooth, battery, the operating system’s power management) and on external services (map, address and route services). Inaccuracies, delays or failures of these factors can lead to incomplete or incorrect recordings and are outside the provider’s sphere of influence.

6. Rights in the app

The provider grants a simple, non-transferable right to use the app. Passing on, copying, modifying or reverse engineering the app is only permitted to the extent mandatory by law.

7. Costs and unlocking of the printout

The app is free to use: recording, viewing, backing up and exporting trips as an Excel, CSV or JSON file costs nothing. The clean, signed PDF printout with verification code and QR code must be unlocked for a fee for each logbook year. Without unlocking a year, the PDF printout carries a watermark (“Test printout, not for the tax office”). The price per logbook year is shown before the purchase in the app and in Google Play’s purchase process and includes statutory value added tax where it applies. As of this version it is €34.99 per logbook year as a one-time payment. There is no subscription and no automatic renewal. The unlocking is permanent for the purchased logbook year. Past logbook years can be purchased at any time, at the price then shown in Google Play; price changes apply only to future purchases.

8. Payment and refunds

The purchase is processed through Google Play. Payment processing, invoice or receipt and refunds are governed by the terms and refund policies of Google Play. When purchasing, Google obtains your consent that the service begins immediately and points out that any right of withdrawal expires as a result. The unlocking takes effect immediately after the purchase. Statutory rights in case of defects remain unaffected. If a payment is refunded by Google, the unlocking of the logbook year concerned ends as soon as the app queries the purchase status at Google Play. The unlocking is tied to the Google account with which the purchase was made; through “Restore purchases” it is restored on a new device with the same account.

9. Verification code

The verification code (checksum and QR code) in the PDF printout makes it possible to check with the app, on the device where the data is stored, whether the trips are unchanged since the printout. It is a technical indication that the data is unchanged and not evidence towards third parties; it says nothing about tax recognition.
